# How the N1 screens read the course

The N1 app teaches from a content bank and lays it out as a plan made from the learner's exam date and daily time.
Every screen in `project/` reads both through `window.ykN1` (`project/n1.js`). No screen holds lesson text of its own.

- `project/n1-content.js` is the bank, generated by `tools/build-content.mjs` from `content/`:
  `{ units, lessons, kanji, mocks }`. A lesson has the shape `content/FORMAT.md` gives, plus `id` (`u3l4`), `unit`,
  `lesson` and `contrast`.
- `project/n1.js` makes and keeps the plan, and builds questions from the bank. Read its comments for the details.
- The plan lives in the app's saved state as `app.plan`. The shell (`yaoki-app/src/Shell.dc.html`) passes it to every
  screen and saves it.

## A screen's head

On the design canvas each screen file runs alone, so it loads what it needs in its `<head>`, after `support.js`:

```html
<script src="./sound.js"></script>
<script src="./n1-content.js"></script>
<script src="./n1.js"></script>
<script src="./audio/clips.js"></script>
```

A board that imports another screen to show it at a moment of its own (ExamResult; MockPaper, MockSheet, MockReading,
MockBreak, MockListening, MockResult and MockAnswers; App) runs that screen's logic, so it loads the same. In the app these are loaded once by the page; the build ignores a screen's `<head>`.

## Reading the plan

```js
const N = window.ykN1;
const app = this.props.app || {};
const plan = app.plan || N.demo();      // on the canvas, or in a test without a plan: the sample plan
const i = N.openDay(plan);               // the day opened on the path, or else the day to work on
const v = N.view(plan, i);               // what that day holds (see view() in n1.js)
```

`v.kind` is `study`, `test` (the week test on every 7th day) or `review` (the days before the exam once everything has
been taught). A study day can hold any of these parts:

- grammar: `v.grammar`, a lesson with 3 patterns, its compare page, 3 drills, a ★ question and 3 exam-style questions;
- kanji and words: `v.kanji` (KANJIDIC2 entries `{c, on, kun, en, s, w: [{w, r, en, x}]}`) and `v.words` (words of the
  lessons: a lesson's own five, then the fifteen of its word bank, `content/BANKS.md`; a word is named
  `w.<lesson id>.<n>`, n from 0, and `N.wordIndex(id, n)` finds its place);
- reading: `v.reading`, an entry of the reading bank `N.readings`: `{ lesson, id (the lesson's), kind, src, item }`.
  `kind` is `short` (the lesson's own item, `FORMAT.md` READING; also in `v.reading.reading`) or `mid`, `info`, `pair`
  (the longer items of `BANKS.md`: `item.passage` is a list of paragraphs or lines, `item.texts` the two texts of a
  pair, `item.lead` an optional line above). `src` is `r.<lesson id>` or `r.<lesson id>.2`;
- listening: `v.listening`, an entry of `N.listenings`: `{ lesson, id, kind, src, item, clips, slow }`. `kind` is `quick`
  (the lesson's own quick-response item, also in `v.listening.listening`, clip `l.<lesson id>`, slow take
  `l.<lesson id>.slow`) or `task`, `point`, `gist` (`BANKS.md`: `item.audio` lists the parts, `clips` their keys
  `l.<lesson id>.<part>` in order; `task` and `point` print the four options, `gist` shows only the numbers 1 to 4 and
  the options are spoken in the `choices` part). `src` is `l.<lesson id>` or `l.<lesson id>.2`.

### Review of the earlier levels

`N.dayReview(plan, i, n)` gives n questions of the N2 to N4 material for day i (`q.review` is true and `q.level` says
the level): the N2 grammar bank, the main app's N2 to N4 course questions and the N2 kanji, in a fixed shuffled order
per plan, taken in turn so each comes once before any comes back. The Scene quiz adds two to the day's quiz; the week
test ends with four (section 復習); a review day's mixed review holds six. A question is of kind sentence (`q.ja` with
its blank or 【underline】), usage (`q.word`, four sentences), order (`q.pre`, `q.post`, four tile options, pick the ★
one, as a 文の文法2 question) or 漢字読み. Its name is `q.src` (`x.…`), and `N.item(src)` rebuilds it for the notebook.

A short plan puts many kanji on a day, and a long one only a few. Leave out any part the day does not hold.
The grammar screens show `N.lessonDay(plan, i)`'s lesson: the open day's, or the nearest earlier day's when the open
day has none. They save progress to that day.

## Saving progress

Progress is kept per day in `plan.days[i].p`. It only ever goes up. Save it with
`this.props.save({ plan: N.mark(plan, i, { g: 1 }) })`. Always build the patch from the plan in the current props.

| key | done when | screens |
|---|---|---|
| `g` | 1: teach pages and compare page read · 2: drills and ★ done · 3: exam-style done | Teach, Compare, Drill, Star, Exam |
| `k` | 1: kanji and words quiz done | Scene |
| `r` | 1: reading done | Reading |
| `l` | 1: listening done | Listening |
| `t` | 1: week test done (test day) | WeekTest |
| `v` | 1: mixed review done (review day) | WeekTest |

`N.done(day)` tells whether a day is complete. A finished day is stamped 済 on the path.

`N.empty(day)` is true for a study day with nothing on it: a missed day whose work a new plan (`N.replan`) moved to
later days. Its `view(...).title` is 予定なし. It is never done or stamped, `N.current` passes over it, the path shows it
as a quiet day that does not open, and Today shows it as a day with nothing left, with the way on to the day to work on.

`N.progress(plan)` counts what has been learnt so far, part by part: `{ g, k, w, r, l }` (grammar lessons with all three
steps, kanji and words with their quiz, reading, listening), the credit a re-planned plan keeps from an earlier calendar
(`plan.past`) included. Count learning with it (Goal, Readiness), not with the stamps or `plan.days` alone.

Results: `N.result(plan, 'tests', wk, { score, of, at })` and `N.result(plan, 'mocks', id, {...})` return a copy of the
plan with the result kept.

## Mistakes

`this.props.miss({ src, wrong })` puts a question in the notebook. `src` names the question, so the notebook can rebuild
it from the bank with `N.item(src)`:

| src | question |
|---|---|
| `k.<kanji index>.<word index>` | a kanji reading |
| `w.<lesson id>.<n>` | a word's own question (n from 0: the lesson's five, then its bank's fifteen) |
| `g.<lesson id>.d0`…`d2` | a drill |
| `g.<lesson id>.s` | the ★ question |
| `g.<lesson id>.x0`…`x2` | an exam-style question |
| `r.<lesson id>`, `r.<lesson id>.2` | a reading item (the lesson's own, its longer one) |
| `l.<lesson id>`, `l.<lesson id>.2` | a listening item (the lesson's own, its longer one) |
| `x.n2.<i>`, `x.<course item id>`, `x.k.<kanji>.<word>` | a review question of the earlier levels |
| `m<mock id>.<item id>` | a mock question |

Mistakes come back for review after 1, 3 and 7 days (`N.due`). They leave the notebook after three right answers in a
row (`N.reviewed`).

## Where each screen goes

| screen | what it shows | next |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | the exam session (`N.sessions()`), daily time (30/45/60), and what that plan covers (`N.preview`). With a plan already made it re-plans (`N.replan`). | Main |
| Main | the week path: the selected week's 7 days with their titles, stamps, today, and the week test. Arrows move between weeks. A tapped day sets `plan.open` (`N.withOpen`). A day ahead of today asks before it opens early. | Today, WeekTest |
| Today | up to 4 cards for the open day's parts, each with its steps. A review day has a mixed-review card. | Teach, Drill, Exam, Scene, Reading, Listening, WeekTest, Main |
| Teach | the 3 patterns, one page each (its own page state) | Compare |
| Compare | the 3 patterns side by side and the quick check; saves `g: 1` | Drill |
| Drill | the 3 drills in turn | Star |
| Star | the ★ question; saves `g: 2` | Today |
| Exam | the 3 exam-style questions on a clock; saves `g: 3` | Today |
| Scene | the day's kanji, then its words, then a quiz (`N.kanjiQuiz` and `N.wordQ`); saves `k: 1` | Today |
| Reading | the day's reading item; saves `r: 1` | Today |
| Listening | the day's listening item; saves `l: 1` | Today |
| WeekTest | `N.testFor(plan, i)`: the week test on a test day, the mixed review on a review day; saves `t` or `v` and the result | Main |
| Notebook | the mistakes (`app.mistakes` through `N.item`), what is due today | Review |
| Review | the due mistakes, one by one, kept with `N.reviewed` | Notebook |
| Mock | the mini mocks (`N.mocks`) and their results | MockRun |
| MockRun | one mini mock, timed by section | Mock |
| Readiness | progress in each part, week test and mock results, days left, and what the plan covers | Notebook, Mock |

## Sound

Clips are named by what they say. `audio/clips.js` lists each clip kept as a file as `{ ms, u }`, and `sound.js` fetches
the file when the clip is first played:

- `g.<lesson id>.<pattern 0-2>.<example 0-1>`: a teach page example;
- `l.<lesson id>`: a listening item (the line, then the three numbered replies);
- `l.<lesson id>.slow`: the same listening item said more slowly (0.8×), for Listening's ゆっくり button;
- `w.<lesson id>.<word 0-19>`: a word and its example sentence (the lesson's five, then the fifteen of its word bank);
- `l.<lesson id>.<part>`: a part of the lesson's longer listening item (`q`, `talk`, `q2`, or `talk`, `choices`);
- `m<mock id>.<n>.<part>`: a part of a listening question of mock 2 or later, where n is the question's place in the
  whole paper (`m2.10.talk`, `m2.12.replies`), kept as a file listed in `audio/clips.js` like the lesson clips
  (`content/MOCKS.md`).

Mock 1 is older: its clips are named in its file (`"clip"`: `m10_q` and so on) and kept in `sound-mock.js`, MP3 as
base64 text. `N.mockQ` gives a mock question's clip names either way.

A screen asks `ykSound.has(key)` and falls back to a silent stand-in when a clip is missing. The stand-in time follows
the length of the text: about 150 ms a character, plus 600 ms. The slow take's stand-in is 1.25 times as long.

## Layout

Every screen is 390 × 844 and must hold every lesson. The limits in `content/FORMAT.md` are what the screens are
designed for. A list that grows with the plan (kanji, the notebook) scrolls inside its own region. Text never runs
out of the frame.
