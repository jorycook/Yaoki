やおき · A JAPANESE LEARNING SUITE FOR THE JLPT

Fall down seven times, get up eight.七転び八起き

Yaoki is a set of small tools for learning Japanese towards the JLPT: courses from N5 to N1 that plan themselves to your exam date, a reader that hides the furigana until you touch a phrase, a typing game for a Japanese keyboard and a speaking game for your phone. Everything runs in the browser, and your progress stays on your own device.

PROTOTYPE
だるま Yaoki, a daruma with one eye painted

Set a goal and he gets one eye. He gets the other when you pass. Knock him over and he gets up again: that is what his name means.

学習の道具 In the study app

The places you come back to every day. These open the app at that place; on your first visit it asks for your goal first.

今週の道The week path

Today's lessons on a route through the week: a grammar lesson, kanji and words, reading or listening, and the week test at the end.

 間違いノートMistake notebook

Every mistake comes back with its explanation, a little further apart each time, until three right answers in a row let it go.

 ミニ模試Mini mock exams

The real paper cut short: both sections in order, one clock, one break, one result. About fifteen minutes.

 合格判定Readiness

An estimate of your score from the week tests, section by section, with the line each section has to clear and the next move.

 書書く練習Writing practice

Each new kanji is written by hand three times: traced, then over faint strokes, then from memory. Stroke order is checked as you go.

 聴聴解Listening

Quick-response and longer listening items with spoken clips, drilled with hints and then answered exam-style against the clock.

読む Reading

Yaoki Reader shows Japanese text with the furigana hidden. Touch a phrase, or hold the mouse on it, and its reading, the words' meanings and any grammar pattern appear; let go and they hide again. Nothing is sent anywhere.

読よむYaoki Reader

Paste any text, up to 30,000 characters, and read it with the readings and meanings a touch away. Texts you add stay in your browser.

遊んで覚える Games

Two games on the same cards as the courses, with the same rewards: a はなまる for a clean answer, a stamp for each day you show up, and Yaoki getting up again every time he falls.

打タイピングYaoki Typing
