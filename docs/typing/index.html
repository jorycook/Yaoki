タイピング　TYPING · JIS KEYBOARD

七転ななころび八起やおき

Fall down seven times, get up eight (八起き, yaoki). Type what is on each card before it reaches Yaoki. If one gets to him, he falls over, gets up again, and the card comes back later in the round.

パソコンとキーボードでどうぞYaoki Typing is made for a computer with a keyboard, in a window at least 700 pixels wide. On a phone or tablet, Yaoki Speaking has you say the words out loud instead.

はじめる前に　Before you play
  • Yaoki Typing is made for a computer with a keyboard. Set it to a Japanese keyboard layout, so the symbols and kana are where the picture shows them.
  • For romaji and symbol keys, switch the input to 英数 (direct input) while you play. Romaji is checked the way the Japanese input method would read it: nn for ん before a vowel, y or n and at the end, wo for を. For kana input the input mode does not matter: the game goes by the key you press.
  • Esc pauses. Backspace takes back the last romaji letter of the card you are typing. A card that waits in front of Yaoki can be skipped with Tab.
  • A card typed with no miss and no hint gets a はなまる on the route, and 連続 counts them in a row. A card that knocked Yaoki over and is typed clean when it comes back is stamped 起. A gold corner marks a card with one of your weak spots. たいへんよくできました on three different days gives a stage its はなまる.
  • Cleared cards ride on to Yaoki, and he jumps, spins, ducks or smashes his way past them (with reduced motion they simply settle).

一時停止　Paused

Nothing moves while this is open. If you quit, the misses so far stay in your weak spots, and the round does not count for your best (自己ベスト). Your typing time so far still counts towards today's stamp.

設定　Settings

ヒント　Romaji and key hints

When stuck: the kana being typed shows after a miss, or after 5 seconds before the first key (3 seconds between keys); a second miss, or as long again, shows the whole spelling. A card where a hint showed is not counted clean, so it gets 済 without a はなまる.

効果音　Sound effects

しずか Quiet: a stamp for each card, a drum when Yaoki falls, a bounce when he gets up, and a sound for each of his tricks. ぜんぶ All: also a tick for each right key and a knock for a miss. A course word's own voice is never covered.

音楽　Music

A tune for each scene, made in the browser. It gets livelier as your clean run grows, drops back when Yaoki falls, and dips while a course word is spoken.

1日の目標　Daily goal

A day's box on the stamp card is stamped once you have typed this long that day. Only typing counts: not pauses, and not more than 8 seconds without a key.
