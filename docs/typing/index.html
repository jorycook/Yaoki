タイピング Yaoki Typing
タイピング　TYPING · JIS KEYBOARD

七転ななころび八起やおき

Fall down seven times, get up eight (八起き, yaoki). Type what is on each card before it reaches Yaoki. If one gets to him, he falls over, gets up again, and the card comes back later in the round.

キーボードをつないでくださいThis game needs a hardware keyboard.

はじめる前に　Before you play
  • Set your computer to a Japanese keyboard layout, so the symbols are where the picture shows them.
  • Switch the input to 英数 (direct input) while you play. What you type is checked the way the Japanese input method would read it: nn for ん before a vowel, y or n and at the end, wo for を.
  • Esc pauses. Backspace takes back the last letter of the card you are typing. A card that waits in front of Yaoki can be skipped with Tab.

一時停止　Paused

Nothing moves while this is open. If you quit, the misses so far stay in your weak spots, and the round does not count for your best (自己ベスト).

ヒント　Romaji and key hints

When stuck: the kana being typed shows after a miss, or after 5 seconds before the first key (3 seconds between keys); a second miss, or as long again, shows the whole spelling.
