Development Group (EDRDG), used in conformance with the Group's licence: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
They are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The JMdict words come from the shortened word list of Yaoki

is offered under the same licence. It is made by tools/make-speaking-readings.py in the Yaoki repository.

== Speech ==
The speech recognition, and the voice that says a word for the player to copy, are the browser's and the device's
own (the Web Speech API): no third-party code is part of this page for them.
