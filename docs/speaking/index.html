はじめる前に　Before you play
  • Hold the phone upright, somewhere quiet, and say each word clearly at a normal pace. The phone's own speech recognition listens (Apple's on an iPhone or iPad, Google's on Android), so it needs the internet; this page keeps nothing you say.
  • The first time, the phone asks to use the microphone: allow it. On an iPhone or iPad, Dictation must be on (Settings › General › Keyboard).
  • The big button at the bottom shows when the phone is listening; tap it to stop or start. In Settings you can choose hold to talk instead.
  • Under the game you see what the phone heard. If it heard something else, say it again: a word said right the first time, with no hint, gets a はなまる, and 連続 counts them in a row.
  • 🔊 きく says the word for you to copy (it counts as a hint, but not on a lesson's new kana). The microphone pauses while it plays, and nothing falls. A card that waits for Yaoki can be skipped.
  • With a screen reader, choose hold to talk in Settings, so the phone does not hear the screen reader reading the card.

    一時停止　Paused

    Nothing moves and nothing is heard while this is open. If you quit, the misses so far stay in your weak spots, and the round does not count for your best (自己ベスト). Your speaking time so far still counts towards today's stamp.

    設定　Settings

    マイク　Microphone

    ずっと聞く Hands-free: the phone listens for the whole round (tap the microphone to stop it for a moment). 押して話す Hold to talk: it listens only while you hold the microphone down.

    ヒント　Readings and romaji

    When stuck: after a miss, or 6 seconds without a word, the reading and its romaji show; a second miss, or 5 seconds more, says the word for you. A card where a hint showed is not counted clean, so it gets 済 without a はなまる.

    効果音　Sound effects

    しずか Quiet: a stamp for each card, a drum when Yaoki falls, a bounce when he gets up, and a sound for each of his tricks. ぜんぶ All: also a knock when the phone heard another word.

    音楽　Music

    A tune for each place, made in the browser. It dips while you speak, so the phone hears you; with the sound up loud, 小さめ Soft or なし Off helps it hear you better.

    1日の目標　Daily goal

    A day's box on the stamp card is stamped once you have played this long that day. Only time speaking counts: not pauses, and not more than 8 seconds without a word.
